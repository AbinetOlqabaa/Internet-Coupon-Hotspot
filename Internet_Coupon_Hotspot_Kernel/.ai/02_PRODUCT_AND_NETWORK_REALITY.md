# Product and Network Reality

Owner sells minute/hour packages. Customer selects package, pays, payment is verified server-side, policy/owner activation is checked, gateway authorizes client, and server/gateway timestamps define the session. Track revenue, usage, customer history, expiry, refunds, badges, bounded bonuses and service quality.

**Critical constraint:** an ordinary Android app cannot universally manage Android's built-in hotspot clients, disconnect a single client, set per-client speed or measure per-client traffic. Support (A) limited owner-management mode with unavailable capabilities clearly disclosed and (B) managed-gateway mode using a compatible router/captive portal/RADIUS or documented gateway API. Never claim enforcement without real gateway confirmation. MAC addresses may change or be spoofed and cannot be the only identity control. A captive portal may be lower-friction than requiring every customer to install an app.

Include customers, packages, coupons/vouchers/QR, payments/ledger, sessions, gateways, notifications, analytics, loyalty, AI copilot, provider settings, audit, export, support and backup. Distinguish measured, estimated and unavailable values.
