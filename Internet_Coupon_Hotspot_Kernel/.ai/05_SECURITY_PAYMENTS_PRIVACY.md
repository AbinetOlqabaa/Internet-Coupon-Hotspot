# Security, Payments and Privacy

Use modern password hashing, secure sessions, owner/resource-level authorization on every API, rate limits, replay protection, input validation, audit and cross-owner isolation tests. Secrets never enter client bundles, source, logs or untrusted LLM prompts. Use provider-hosted/tokenized payments; never store raw card data. Verify signed webhooks or server-side provider status, timestamps, idempotency and amount/currency. Client success screens do not prove payment. Manual cash confirmation records actor, time, amount and reference.

Gateway credentials use least privilege and encrypted transport; commands have timeouts, retries, idempotency and reconciliation. Collect minimal customer data, disclose purpose/retention, support appropriate export/deletion, and avoid browsing-history/traffic-content inspection. Redact PII and secrets in AI context and logs.
