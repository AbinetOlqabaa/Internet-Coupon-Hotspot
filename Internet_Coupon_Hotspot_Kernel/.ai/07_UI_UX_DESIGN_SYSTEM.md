# UI/UX and Design System

Modern, calm operations UI: deep navy, teal actions, warm amber accent, neutral surfaces, restrained shadows and legible analytics. Centralize brand/status colors, typography, spacing, radii, elevation and breakpoints in shared tokens; no scattered literal colors. Semantic status colors stay separate from brand colors.

Owner navigation: Overview, Customers, Packages/Pricing, Coupons, Payments, Live Sessions, Network/Gateway, Analytics, Loyalty, AI Assistant, Notifications, Settings, Audit/Exports and Help. Customer flow: offers, payment state, activation progress, remaining time, measured usage if available, session history, receipt and support.

Support 320px+ phones, landscape, tablets and desktop. No page-wide horizontal overflow; wide tables scroll within their container. Support font scaling, safe areas, touch targets, keyboard, Android back navigation, accessible charts with tabular alternatives, loading/empty/offline/error/retry states, and visible measured-vs-estimated labels. Shared components include AppShell, PageHeader, StatusBadge, MetricCard, ResponsiveTable, FilterBar, ConfirmDialog, FormField, EmptyState, ErrorState, LoadingSkeleton, NotificationCenter, ChartPanel, MoneyDisplay, DurationDisplay, CapabilityBadge and AuditTimeline.
